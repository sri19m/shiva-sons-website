# SHIVA SONS TECHNOLOGIES
## Company Website & Business Development Roadmap

> **Mission:** Build a credible technology company, enter the market through services and opportunities such as SaskTenders, learn from real customers, and identify scalable problems that can eventually become products/SaaS.

---

## 1. Current Strategy

We are a two-person founding team with limited time:

- **Rahul:** Full-time job, Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM
- **Friend:** College student, classes Monday–Friday, 9 AM–3 PM

Therefore, our strategy is:

**Start small → build credibility → learn the market → win initial work → identify repeated problems → build scalable products.**

We are **not** trying to build everything at once.

### Our priorities

1. Establish Shiva Sons Technologies professionally.
2. Build and launch the company website.
3. Define and continuously improve our service offerings.
4. Register and learn the SaskTenders/GEM procurement process.
5. Selectively pursue suitable government opportunities.
6. Explore private B2B opportunities.
7. Identify repeated customer problems.
8. Turn promising repeated problems into internal tools/products.
9. Eventually build scalable technology/SaaS products.

# 11. Service Discovery

We should NOT list every possible service just because we can technically provide it.

Instead, create a service matrix.

### Candidate categories

- IT Support
- Microsoft 365 Support
- Hardware & Software Setup
- Network Setup
- Cloud Services
- Cybersecurity
- Website Development
- Software Development
- AI Implementation
- Business Automation
- Excel/Data Automation
- Reporting & Dashboards
- IT Consulting
- Hardware Procurement
- Technology Consulting

Each service should be evaluated before being added to the website.

### Service evaluation

| Factor | Question |
|---|---|
| Customer demand | Are businesses actually asking for it? |
| Our capability | Can we deliver it well? |
| Startup cost | How much money/equipment is required? |
| Margin | Can we make reasonable profit? |
| Competition | How crowded is the market? |
| Tender potential | Does government buy this? |
| Scalability | Could this eventually become a product? |
| Founder fit | Can two people deliver it? |
| Repeatability | Can the process be standardized? |

# 20. Working Principles

### Principle 1 — Don't build before understanding

> Problem → Customer → Solution → Prototype → Product

Not:

> Tool → Website → App → Find customer

### Principle 2 — Don't subscribe to everything

Use free tools first.

Upgrade only when a paid tool clearly saves time or creates value.

### Principle 3 — Don't chase every opportunity

Focus on opportunities where Shiva Sons can realistically compete.

### Principle 4 — Services can fund discovery

A service business can help us learn the market and finance future products.

### Principle 5 — Keep the long-term vision big

We don't need to know the final product today.

The company can evolve.

### Principle 6 — Two founders, limited time

Every activity should be evaluated by:

> **Does this meaningfully move Shiva Sons forward?**

## Final Direction

**Shiva Sons Technologies is not being built around one predetermined product.**

We are building the company infrastructure first.

Then:

**Establish → Enter → Learn → Deliver → Discover → Productize → Scale**

The long-term objective is to identify a sufficiently painful and widespread problem that can become a scalable technology business.
